+ TRENDING FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1

What is a multitenancy software and how does it work?

Multitenancy is an architecture where a single instance of a software application serves multiple customers (tenants). Each tenant's data is isolated and remains invisible to other tenants, ensuring full security and privacy while sharing the same underlying infrastructure.
Q2

Can I connect my company's custom domain to my panel?

Yes, absolutely. Axiolance allows you to easily connect your own custom domains (e.g., pos.yourcompany.com). Once you point your DNS records to our servers, we automatically handle SSL certificate provisioning and subdomain routing for you.
Q3

How does the integrated Stripe billing manage subscriptions?

Our Stripe integration manages recurring subscriptions automatically. You can set monthly cycles, allocate metered feature limits, charge setups fees, and print professional invoices. Tenants can access their billing portal to change payment cards or upgrade licenses instantly.
Q4

Is my accounting and sales data securely backed up?

Data safety is our primary focus. Axiolance replicates all databases across secure geographical storage arrays daily. We encrypt databases at rest and protect access points using HTTPS encryption tunnels to prevent vulnerabilities.
Q5

Do you offer translation and currency custom configurations?

Yes, our Multilingual and Multicurrency settings allow teams to deploy local interfaces, convert tax levels dynamically, issue invoices in regional currencies, and toggle translation tables easily from their admin panels.
