How long does a Dynamics 365 implementation take?
Typical Dynamics 365 projects range from 3 to 12 months depending on organizational complexity, number of modules deployed, custom extensions, and data migration scope.
Can you migrate from Microsoft Dynamics AX?
Yes. We help organizations assess, plan, and migrate from legacy Dynamics AX environments to modern Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Supply Chain Management cloud solutions with zero data loss.
Can Dynamics 365 integrate with Microsoft 365?
Yes. Dynamics 365 natively integrates with Microsoft 365 applications including Outlook, Teams, Excel, SharePoint, and Power BI, significantly improving cross-departmental collaboration and productivity.
Do you provide AI solutions?
Yes. VGT offers AI-powered automation, chatbots, predictive analytics, document processing, and business intelligence solutions that complement Microsoft technologies and Copilot.
